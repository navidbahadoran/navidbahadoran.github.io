Current Research

Research in high-dimensional statistics, econometrics, low-rank models, factor methods, and leakage-aware validation.

Ongoing research · 2026–Present

Debiased Inference for Dynamic Panels with Low-Rank Coefficient Heterogeneity

Dynamic PanelsLow-Rank ModelsEconometricsInference
  • Developing dynamic-panel models in which autoregressive and slope coefficients vary across units and time while each coefficient matrix has its own low-rank structure.
  • Combining nuclear-norm rank screening, unpenalized fixed-rank refits, target-specific inference, and split-panel bias correction under cross-sectional dependence.
  • Studying finite-sample behavior through Monte Carlo experiments and empirical applications to regional unemployment and house-price momentum.
Ongoing research · 2026–Present

Leakage-Aware Target Shrinkage of Principal Directions in Fixed-Sample High-Dimensional Factor Models

PCAFactor ModelsTarget LeakageCross-Validation
  • Studying persistent directional error in high-dimensional PCA and separating population alignment from same-sample target leakage.
  • Developing target-shrinkage and orthogonal calibration methods together with repeated out-of-fold selection and refit-stability analysis.
  • Evaluating the methods through simulations and a rolling U.S. macroeconomic application using purged tuning and test blocks.

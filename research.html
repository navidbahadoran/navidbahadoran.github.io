Research & Publications

Current Research and Working Papers
  • Debiased Inference for Dynamic Panels with Low-Rank Coefficient Heterogeneity: developing high-dimensional dynamic-panel models with heterogeneous coefficients, latent common factors, and cross-sectional dependence.
  • Developing low-rank estimation, rank-selection, and bias-corrected inference methods, with Monte Carlo experiments for finite-sample performance and dependence-robust inference.
  • Leakage-Aware Target Shrinkage of Principal Directions in Fixed-Sample High-Dimensional Factor Models: studying PCA estimation error, target leakage, and shrinkage of ordered principal directions.
  • Designing repeated out-of-fold and purged validation procedures and evaluating out-of-sample reconstruction, refit stability, simulations, and rolling U.S. macroeconomic applications.

Econometrics

Debiased Inference for Dynamic Panels with Low-Rank Coefficient Heterogeneity

This project develops estimation and inference for dynamic panel models in which autoregressive and observed-covariate effects may vary across both units and time. Each coefficient matrix is allowed to have its own low-rank structure, while interactive effects capture additional latent common variation.

Research contributions

  • Develops a post-regularization architecture that uses nuclear-norm screening to propose rank structures and unpenalized fixed-rank refits for final estimation.
  • Constructs target-specific inference with Riesz representers and a two-way split-panel correction for leading loading- and factor-estimation biases.
  • Allows dynamic feedback, predetermined covariates, conditional heteroskedasticity, and spatial or cross-sectional dependence with dependence-robust variance estimation.
  • Studies finite-sample performance through Monte Carlo experiments and empirical applications to regional unemployment and house-price momentum.

Working Paper · 2026–Present
High-Dimensional Statistics

Leakage-Aware Target Shrinkage of Principal Directions in Fixed-Sample High-Dimensional Factor Models

This project studies principal-direction estimation when the cross-sectional dimension is large but the estimation window is short. It separates persistent PCA directional error from same-sample target leakage and develops shrinkage and alignment procedures for ordered principal directions.

Research contributions

  • Decomposes fixed-sample PCA error into out-of-factor-space error and within-space rotation under a general positive-definite idiosyncratic Gram limit.
  • Develops leakage-aware target shrinkage with polar normalization, distinguishing genuine population alignment from agreement created by shared sample noise.
  • Uses repeated out-of-fold reconstruction for candidate selection and studies fold-to-full refit instability and direction-sensitive Procrustes alignment.
  • Evaluates the proposed procedures through simulations and a rolling U.S. macroeconomic application using previous-window targets with purged tuning and test blocks.

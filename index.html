Quantitative Research · Financial Mathematics

High-dimensional statistics, econometrics, factor models, and financial risk

I am a PhD candidate in Applied Mathematics (Financial Mathematics) at Florida State University, expected to graduate in May 2028. My research develops statistical methods for high-dimensional financial and economic data, with current work on low-rank dynamic-panel inference and leakage-aware PCA and factor models.

Before my doctoral studies, I worked in institutional investment risk at the State of Wisconsin Investment Board and in quantitative research at Massachusetts PRIM, building Python- and SQL-based tools for portfolio risk, liquidity analytics, and predictive investment research.

My interests include quantitative research, model risk and validation, portfolio and treasury analytics, time-series econometrics, and computational finance.
